<p class="lede">Computational materials scientist specialising in <em>ab initio</em> simulations and machine learning for atomistic modelling, with research centred on 2D materials and energy systems. Over ten years of research experience, nine years of university teaching, and more than thirty publications in journals including <em>Nature Communications</em>, <em>Science Advances</em>, <em>Nano Letters</em> and <em>Physical Review Letters</em>.</p>
